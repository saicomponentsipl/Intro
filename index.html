🏢 About Sai Components India

Sai Components India Private Limited – Karnataka's exclusive branch office for Rexnord Fans. Supplying to industries for over 2.5 decades, we serve MNCs, PSUs & manufacturers across automation, electrical panel builders, chiller manufacturers, kitchen equipment, ducting, defence & more.

We supply Fans, Motors & Grills – exhaust fans, axial fans, large axial fans, chiller motors, impellers, fan guards/grills, also domestic & commercial exhaust fans. We are a trusted supplier of electronic components for electronics manufacturing, EV, defence & advanced technology sectors. Known for quality products, dependable supply & trusted service.
